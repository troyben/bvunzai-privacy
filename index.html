Privacy Policy

Bvunzai Halfnumbers · Effective 8 October 2026

Bvunzai Halfnumbers provides quick access to local service directories, contact numbers, coded dialing, utilities, travel information, and emergency contacts. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

Bvunzai Halfnumbers does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Calls and external links

When you choose to call a number or open a website, map, social-media page, picture, or WhatsApp link, the app hands that request to the relevant app or service on your device. Bvunzai Halfnumbers does not receive or store the information you provide to those third-party services. Their own privacy policies apply.

Advertising

The app may display locally bundled promotional images or videos for local services. These promotions do not track users, collect personal information, or use an advertising network SDK.

Device permissions

The app does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or call history. Selecting a phone number opens your device's dialing interface so that you remain in control of whether a call is placed.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Because no personal information is collected, the app does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before the relevant app update is released.
